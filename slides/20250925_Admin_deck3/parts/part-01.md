<div class="page-title">よい例と“じゃない”例／Dos and Don'ts</div>

# <span style="color:var(--accent-dark)">よい例と“じゃない”例</span>／Dos and Don'ts

<div style="display:flex; gap:22px; align-items:stretch; margin-top:8px;">

<div class="pcard" style="flex:1;">
<div class="pc-h" style="background:#777;">好ましくない例／Common Pitfalls</div>
<ul style="font-size:20px;">
<li>誰かの一人の研究テーマを発展させる<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Just expanding on a member's personal research.</span></li>
<li>課題解決ではなく研究する<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Treating this as a pure academic research project.</span></li>
<li>ビジコンとして捉える<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Mistaking this event as a business plan competition.</span></li>
<li>専門性と全く無関係な話になる<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">An idea that ignores team's collective expertise.<br>(Please avoid a generic solution anyone could think of.)</span></li>
<li>具体性のない精神論<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Just providing a solution that's just a slogan.</span></li>
<li>壮大すぎるテーマ設定<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Choosing a problem that's too broad</span></li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="pcard" style="flex:1;">
<div class="pc-h">よい例／Dos</div>
<ul style="font-size:20px;">
<li>「私達だから思いついた！」が光る解決策<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Perspective-Shifting Ideas with "Why Us?" Solutions</span></li>
<li>研究室の外を想像するもの<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Grounding Your Idea: Think Beyond the Lab.</span></li>
<li>視点を変える面白いアイデア<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Unconventional &amp; Humorous ideas.</span></li>
<li>状況を定義したり、仮定するもの<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Feel free to set your own "What Ifs" or definitions.</span></li>
<li>創発的な気づきや小さな発見<br><span style="color:#666; font-size:18px;">Small yet emergent insights that can only arise when diverse PhD candidates come together.</span></li>
</ul>
</div>

</div>

<div class="box-accent pip-safe" style="margin-top:14px;"><b>皆さんの専門分野が交差するからこそ生まれる、世界やものごとの見え方が変わる話題を求めます</b><br><span style="font-size:20px;">We will evaluate ideas born from the interactions of your disciplines, that have the power to change how we see the world or things.</span></div>
