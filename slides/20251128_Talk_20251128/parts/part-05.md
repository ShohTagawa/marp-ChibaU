# <span style="color:var(--accent-dark)">学生の感想</span><span style="font-size:24px; font-weight:700; color:#888; margin-left:16px;">3 AIとの関わり方</span>

<div class="box-info" style="margin-top:10px; font-size:22px; line-height:1.6;">
